:::project-section{type="text"}
Istgah (Persian for station) is a React Native app for exploring public transit in Iran. Metro, BRT, and bus stops sit on one map, with search, station details, and routing from your location.

Six cities are supported (Tehran, Isfahan, Mashhad, Tabriz, Karaj, Shiraz). Toggle layers, switch street/satellite basemaps, and use Persian (RTL) or English (LTR) with light and dark themes. Bus and BRT stops can trigger an arrival inquiry via USSD.

Available on [Cafe Bazaar](http://cafebazaar.ir/app/?id=com.mohsendastaran.istgah&ref=share) and [Myket](https://myket.ir/app/com.mohsendastaran.istgah).
