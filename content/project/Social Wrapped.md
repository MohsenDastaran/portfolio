:::project-section{type="text"}
Social Wrapped reviews official social media and platform exports on your device. Telegram, Google ZIPs, Instagram archives, and similar files are parsed locally — nothing is uploaded.

Use it in the [browser](https://wrapped.dastaran.com/) or [download](https://github.com/MohsenDastaran/social-wrapped/releases/latest) the Windows, macOS, Linux, or Android app. Desktop builds work offline; Android APKs ship without internet permission. No account, passwords, or OAuth — pick a file, parse it in-app, and keep results on this device.

Supported imports include Telegram, WhatsApp, X, Google Takeout, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Spotify, Apple Music, LinkedIn, and ChatGPT.
